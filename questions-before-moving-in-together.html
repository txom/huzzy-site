19 questions to ask before moving in together

19 questions · Updated November 2026

Moving in together is exciting and a little terrifying. The small stuff, like who does the dishes and how much alone time each of you needs, causes more trouble than the big stuff. Talk before the lease, not after.

Home life

  1. What does a clean home mean to you?
  2. How would we split the chores in a way that feels fair?
  3. What is one thing about your home habits that I should know now?
  4. How do you feel about guests dropping by?
  5. How much alone time do you need at home?
  6. What is your sleep schedule, honestly?
  7. What would be a dealbreaker in a shared home?

Money

  1. How would we split rent and bills?
  2. Do we have separate accounts, a joint one, or both?
  3. How much debt does each of us have?
  4. How do we decide on bigger purchases for the home?
  5. What does saving look like for us?
  6. What happens if one of us loses their job?

Us

  1. What are you most excited about?
  2. What are you most nervous about?
  3. How will we keep dating after we move in?
  4. What do you need from me when we argue in the same space?
  5. What will we do if it is not working?
  6. What does "ours" mean to you?

A tip before you start

Write the answers down. It sounds like a lot, but a one-page note about chores, money and space saves a lot of fights later.

Questions people ask

How long should you date before moving in together?

There is no magic number. What matters is that you have talked about money, space and chores, and both feel ready.

What do couples fight about most after moving in?

Chores and money, almost always. A fair split you both agreed to out loud prevents most of it.
