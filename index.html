Questions

Is it free?

The daily question, the streak and half of the decks are free. Huzzy Plus opens everything and one plan covers both of you.

Does my partner need the app?

Yes, you each answer on your own phone. You can also use the face-to-face decks with one phone and no account.

Who sees my answers?

Only the two of you, and only after you both answer. Never anyone else.

Is it only for new couples?

No. There are decks for couples just starting, for years together, for long distance and for reconnecting.
