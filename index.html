Questions

Is it free?

Yes to start. The Appetizer deck (84 cards) and your streak are free. The other decks are paid, one by one or all with Huzzy Plus, and one purchase covers both of you.

How do I sign in?

With your Google account (no passwords) or without an account. You type your name and get a link to send to your partner, who joins with it. With Google your account comes with you if you change phones. Without an account, it lives only on that phone.

Does my partner need the app?

Yes, you each answer on your own phone. You can also play a deck face to face with one phone.

Who sees my answers?

Only the two of you, and only after you both answer. Never anyone else.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. There are no ads and we never sell your data or share it for advertising. You can delete your account and answers any time.

Is it only for new couples?

No. There are decks for couples just starting, for years together, for long distance and for reconnecting.
