23 funny questions for couples

23 questions · Updated November 2026

Laughing together is underrated relationship glue. These questions are silly on purpose. Nobody is allowed to answer seriously. Judging the answers is half the fun.

Absolute nonsense

  1. What would be the worst possible name for a pet?
  2. Which of us would survive longer in a zombie movie?
  3. What is the most ridiculous thing you have ever argued about?
  4. If our lives were a sitcom, what would it be called?
  5. Which animal would you be reincarnated as, and why?
  6. What would your villain origin story be?
  7. Which cartoon character are you secretly most like?

Roast each other (gently)

  1. What is my most embarrassing dance move?
  2. Who is worse at parking, you or me?
  3. What would my last words be if they were completely on brand?
  4. What do I say way too often?
  5. What is the thing I think I am great at but am not?
  6. Who falls asleep first during movies?
  7. What would be on my tombstone if you wrote it?

Would you rather

  1. Would you rather always have to sing instead of talk, or always have to dance instead of walk?
  2. Would you rather give up cheese or give up coffee?
  3. Would you rather text me only in emojis or only in song lyrics?
  4. Would you rather fight one horse-sized duck or a hundred duck-sized horses?
  5. Would you rather have a rewind button or a pause button for your life?

Our own lore

  1. What is our best inside joke?
  2. What is the funniest thing that has happened on one of our dates?
  3. What is the worst gift we have ever given each other?
  4. What nickname for me would you never say in public?

A tip before you start

Keep score. Whoever gets the biggest laugh wins the right to pick the next movie. Silly stakes make people relax, and relaxed people answer more honestly.

Questions people ask

What are funny questions to ask your boyfriend or girlfriend?

The would-you-rather and the roast-each-other ones work for both. Keep it kind, and stop if one of you stops laughing.

Are these good for a first date?

Yes, as long as you are both comfortable. They break the ice fast and nobody has to say anything heavy.
