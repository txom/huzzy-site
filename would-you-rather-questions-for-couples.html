23 would you rather questions for couples

23 questions · Updated November 2026

Would you rather is the easiest game to play with a partner because nobody has to think up a question. You just pick a side and defend it. The justifications are where you learn the most.

Romantic

  1. Would you rather have a surprise weekend away or a perfect night at home?
  2. Would you rather get a handwritten letter or a long voice message?
  3. Would you rather slow dance in the kitchen or watch the sunrise together?
  4. Would you rather get breakfast in bed or a massage?
  5. Would you rather get a spontaneous kiss in public or a love note in your pocket?
  6. Would you rather cook together or order in and cuddle?
  7. Would you rather travel to a new place together or go back to our favorite one?

Funny

  1. Would you rather never use your phone in bed or never use the TV in the living room?
  2. Would you rather share one toothbrush or one pair of underwear for a week?
  3. Would you rather have your search history read aloud or your texts read aloud?
  4. Would you rather always say what you think or never say what you think?
  5. Would you rather be stuck in an elevator with an ex or with my whole family?
  6. Would you rather only eat sweet food or only eat savory food forever?
  7. Would you rather lose your sense of smell or your sense of taste?

Real life

  1. Would you rather save money for a house or spend it on experiences?
  2. Would you rather live in a tiny place in a great city or a huge place in the middle of nowhere?
  3. Would you rather have a big wedding or elope somewhere beautiful?
  4. Would you rather be rich and busy or comfortable and free?
  5. Would you rather have one great friend group in common or separate ones we each love?
  6. Would you rather have a pet or a plant first?
  7. Would you rather always be five minutes early or always be fifteen minutes late?

A little spicy

  1. Would you rather be teased or be complimented all night?
  2. Would you rather text flirty things or say them out loud?

A tip before you start

Make everyone pick a side and explain why. The question is the excuse. The reasons are the conversation.

Are would you rather questions good for couples?

Yes. They are low pressure and they show you how your partner thinks, what they value and how they handle a hard choice.

How many should we ask in one night?

Five to ten is plenty. Stop when the laughing slows down.
