22 questions to ask a new partner

22 questions · Updated November 2026

The first few months are for finding out who this person really is. You do not need an interview. You need a few good questions asked at the right time. These are friendly and a little curious, and none of them are a trap.

Getting to know you

  1. What does a perfect lazy Sunday look like for you?
  2. What is something you could talk about for hours?
  3. What was your favorite thing about growing up where you did?
  4. What is a small thing you are really proud of?
  5. What is your most-used app besides messages?
  6. What is your relationship with your family like?
  7. Who is the person you call when something great happens?

How you love

  1. How do you like to be shown affection?
  2. What does feeling cared for look like to you?
  3. What is something that makes you feel anxious in a new relationship?
  4. How do you like to handle an argument? Talk right away or take space?
  5. What is a green flag for you in a partner?
  6. What did your last relationship teach you?
  7. How much alone time do you need in a week?

What you want

  1. What are you looking for right now?
  2. What does a good relationship feel like to you?
  3. Where do you want to be in a few years?
  4. What do you want your daily life to feel like?
  5. What is non-negotiable for you in a partner?
  6. What is something you want to try this year?
  7. What would make you feel secure with me?
  8. What is the best part of getting to know me so far?

A tip before you start

Spread these across weeks, not one night. Share your own answer each time, and notice how the other person reacts to yours. How they listen tells you as much as what they say.

Questions people ask

What should you ask someone in the first month of dating?

Stay light and curious. Ask about what they love, what they do on a normal day and how they like to spend time. Leave the heavy topics for later.

When is the right time to talk about what we are?

When it starts to matter to one of you. A simple "what are you looking for?" is a respectful way to open it.
