20 questions to reconnect with your partner

20 questions · Updated November 2026

Sometimes you do not fight, you just drift. Work, kids, phones, and suddenly you are two roommates. You do not need a retreat. You need a few honest questions, asked gently.

Start soft

  1. What is one thing from your day you would like me to know?
  2. What is something small I did recently that you appreciated?
  3. What is a moment this week when you felt close to me?
  4. What song would you put on if we had the evening to ourselves?
  5. What is something that made you laugh today?
  6. What was your favorite thing we used to do that we do not anymore?
  7. What is something you miss about us?

Be honest

  1. When do you feel the most distant from me?
  2. What do you need more of from me right now?
  3. What is something I do that makes you feel unheard?
  4. What would make you feel more cared for this week?
  5. Is there something you have been holding back?
  6. What are you tired of carrying alone?
  7. What can I take off your plate?

Plan something

  1. What is one thing we could do together this weekend?
  2. What would make a Tuesday night feel special?
  3. What is a tiny habit we could start every day?
  4. What would you like us to try that we have never done?
  5. What is one way we can show up for each other this month?
  6. What are we going to do to protect our time together?

A tip before you start

Pick the time, not the topic. Ten minutes after dinner with no screens is enough. Do not turn a question into a complaint. If it gets heated, pause and come back to it tomorrow.

Questions people ask

How do you reconnect with a partner you have drifted from?

Start with tiny, regular moments of attention. A real question every day, put into a routine, does more than a big gesture.

Should we see a therapist?

If you are stuck, hurting or it keeps getting worse, yes. A therapist is a good idea for any couple who wants help. These questions are a starting point, not a replacement.
