24 deep questions for couples

24 questions · Updated November 2026

Deep does not have to mean heavy. The best deep questions are simple ones that people have just never been asked. Pick a quiet moment, put the phones away and take turns.

Who you are

  1. What do you wish people understood about you without you having to explain?
  2. When did you last feel truly proud of yourself?
  3. What are you afraid you are not good at?
  4. What part of your childhood do you still carry around?
  5. What did you believe at 20 that you no longer believe?
  6. What do you do when you want to feel safe?
  7. What would you tell your younger self?

Who we are together

  1. What is something I have helped you grow into?
  2. When did you feel most supported by me?
  3. Is there something between us that we avoid talking about?
  4. What do we do really well as a team?
  5. What is a moment in our relationship you replay in your head?
  6. What do you need from me that you have not asked for yet?
  7. What is one small thing that would make us closer this month?

Where we are going

  1. What does a good ordinary day look like for us in ten years?
  2. What is a risk you would want us to take together?
  3. What do you want to be true about our home?
  4. How do you want us to handle hard times when they come?
  5. What kind of couple do you hope people see when they see us?
  6. What would you regret not doing together?
  7. What is one thing you would like us to stop doing?

The 3 a.m. ones

  1. What scares you most about losing someone?
  2. What do you think happens after we die?
  3. What is a secret hope you have for your life?

A tip before you start

There is no right answer, only honest ones. If your partner shares something tender, thank them before you react. A simple "thank you for telling me" keeps the door open for the next one.

Questions people ask

How do you start a deep conversation with your partner?

Ask one question and share your own answer first. It makes it feel safer, and it shows you are not grading them.

Can deep questions help a relationship that feels distant?

They can open the door, but they work best as a habit. A short question every day does more than one long talk after a fight.
