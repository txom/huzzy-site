25 long distance relationship questions

25 questions · Updated November 2026

Long distance runs on conversation. When you cannot share a couch, you share questions. These help calls feel like time spent together and not like a status update.

Staying close

  1. What small thing makes you feel close to me when we are apart?
  2. What do you miss most about me right now?
  3. What is the first thing we do when we see each other again?
  4. What is one thing from your day you wish I had been there for?
  5. What song makes you think of us?
  6. What is a tiny ritual we could start for our calls?
  7. What is the best part of our distance, if there is one?

Trust and honesty

  1. What is the hardest part of being apart for you?
  2. When do you feel the most insecure? Can I help?
  3. What do you need from me when you are having a bad day far away?
  4. What rule should we have for calls and texts?
  5. How do we want to handle hard conversations when we cannot hug afterward?
  6. What would make you feel more connected?
  7. What do we need to talk about before the next visit?

Future and plans

  1. Where do you picture us ending up?
  2. What would be the first thing you would do if we lived in the same city?
  3. What are we doing to make the distance shorter?
  4. What is a place you want us to visit together?
  5. What does our first week together look like?
  6. How do you imagine a regular Tuesday when we are together?

Just for fun

  1. What is the weirdest thing you have seen this week?
  2. What movie should we watch at the same time tonight?
  3. What is one thing you want to show me on camera right now?
  4. What would you cook for me if you could?
  5. What is your most embarrassing moment this month?

A tip before you start

Pick one question before the call so you both have time to think. A question you have thought about gets a better answer than one that lands out of nowhere.

Questions people ask

What should we talk about on long distance calls?

Mix small daily things with one real question. The daily things make it normal. The question makes it count.

How often should long distance couples talk?

As often as works for both. Short and regular beats long and rare.
