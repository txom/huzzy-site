22 date night questions

22 questions · Updated November 2026

You booked the table, you got dressed, and then you spent forty minutes talking about the dishwasher. These questions are for the nights when you want dinner to feel like a date again.

Start the night

  1. What was the best part of your week?
  2. What is something you are looking forward to?
  3. What made you smile today that I do not know about?
  4. What is a tiny win you had recently?
  5. If tonight had a theme song, what would it be?
  6. What is something you have been wanting to tell me?
  7. What is the best thing we have eaten together?

Remember us

  1. What is your favorite date we have ever had?
  2. What is a trip we took that you think about often?
  3. What is the funniest thing that has happened to us?
  4. What did you think of me on our first date?
  5. What is something I do that you still love after all this time?
  6. What was the moment you realized we were serious?
  7. What is a place we should go back to?

Look ahead

  1. What is one thing we should do before the year ends?
  2. What is a dream you have never told me about?
  3. What is a skill you want us to learn together?
  4. If we could change one thing about our routine, what would it be?
  5. What is a trip you want us to take in the next five years?
  6. What would a perfect weekend look like for us?

Dessert questions

  1. What is one thing you would like me to do more often?
  2. What is one thing you want me to know today?

A tip before you start

Phones in a pile in the middle of the table, and the first one to grab theirs picks up the check. It sounds silly and it works.

Questions people ask

What do you talk about on a date night with your spouse?

Start with the good things from the week, then remember something fun you did together and finish with something you want to do next.

How do you avoid awkward silences on a date?

Keep two or three questions in your pocket. A silence with a question waiting is comfortable. A silence with nothing is awkward.
