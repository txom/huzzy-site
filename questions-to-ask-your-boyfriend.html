25 questions to ask your boyfriend

25 questions · Updated November 2026

Asking "how was your day?" for the fifth night in a row gets you "fine." These questions get you stories. Pick a few, ask them over dinner or on a walk, and then actually listen to the answer.

Easy and fun

  1. What's the most useless talent you have?
  2. What song do you secretly love but never admit to?
  3. What would you eat every day for a week and never get tired of?
  4. What's your go-to excuse when you want to leave a party early?
  5. Which of my habits do you find most annoying but also kind of cute?
  6. If we won a free trip tomorrow, where would you pick?
  7. What's the last thing that made you laugh until it hurt?

About us

  1. What was the first thing you noticed about me?
  2. When did you know you really liked me?
  3. What's your favorite thing we do together that costs nothing?
  4. What do I do that makes you feel loved?
  5. Is there something small I could do more often that would make your week?
  6. What's one thing you've never told me because it felt silly?
  7. What was your first impression of me, honestly?

A little deeper

  1. What are you most proud of that almost nobody knows about?
  2. When do you feel most like yourself?
  3. What do you worry about that you rarely say out loud?
  4. What did you need as a kid that you did not get?
  5. How do you like to be comforted when you are stressed?
  6. What would you want to do more of in life if money did not matter?
  7. What is something you want us to try this year?

Just a little cheeky

  1. What is the most romantic thing you can imagine me doing for you?
  2. What outfit of mine makes you lose your train of thought?
  3. What is your guilty pleasure that you hope I never find out about?
  4. If we had a whole free day with no phones, how would you spend it with me?

A tip before you start

Do not fire all thirty at once. One question, a real answer and a follow-up like "why?" beats ten quick ones. If he answers with one word, try "tell me more" before you move on.

Questions people ask

What are good questions to ask your boyfriend over text?

The short, playful ones work best over text. Try the easy and fun group, like the useless talent or the song he secretly loves. Save the deeper ones for when you can see each other.

How often should we ask each other questions like these?

Once a day is plenty. A small habit you actually keep beats a long deep talk you do once a year.
