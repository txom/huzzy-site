22 questions to ask your husband or wife

22 questions · Updated November 2026

After years together you know the coffee order and the sleep position. But people keep changing, and the person across the table is not who they were five years ago. Stay curious. These questions help.

Who are you now

  1. What is something you have changed your mind about in the last few years?
  2. What do you want to learn right now?
  3. What has surprised you about getting older?
  4. What is something you are proud of that you rarely mention?
  5. What do you wish we had more time for?
  6. What worries you at 2 a.m. lately?
  7. What is your favorite part of your day?

Us, after all this time

  1. What is something I do that you still notice and love?
  2. What do you think we are better at than when we started?
  3. What was the hardest year of our marriage, and what helped?
  4. What small ritual of ours would you hate to lose?
  5. What would you like to stop arguing about?
  6. What is a memory of us that you can still feel?
  7. How can I make your week easier?

What is next

  1. What do you want our next ten years to look like?
  2. What is something on your list that you have never told me?
  3. What do you want us to do while we are still able?
  4. What does a good retirement look like to you?
  5. How do you want us to take care of each other when we are old?
  6. What kind of grandparents or elders do you hope we become?
  7. What would a perfect anniversary look like?
  8. What do you hope people say about us?

A tip before you start

Pick one that surprises you both. If you already know the answer, ask it anyway and compare. The gap between what you guessed and what they said is where the good conversation lives.

Questions people ask

How do you reconnect with your spouse after years together?

Small and often. One good question a day beats a big romantic gesture once a year.

What if we have run out of things to talk about?

That usually means you stopped being curious, not that there is nothing left. Questions bring it back.
