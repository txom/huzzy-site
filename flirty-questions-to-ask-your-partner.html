21 flirty questions to ask your partner

21 questions · Updated November 2026

Flirting does not stop when you move in together. If anything, it gets better. These questions are playful and a little cheeky, the kind you ask with a smile and a raised eyebrow.

Sweet and teasing

  1. What is your favorite thing about the way I look at you?
  2. What is the sexiest thing about me that has nothing to do with my looks?
  3. What song makes you think of me in a way you would not say in front of your mom?
  4. What is the best kiss we have ever had?
  5. Where is the most unexpected place you have wanted to kiss me?
  6. What did you think the first time you saw me really dressed up?
  7. What do I do that drives you a little wild?

Cheeky

  1. What is the naughtiest text you almost sent me?
  2. What would you love me to whisper in your ear?
  3. What is your favorite way to wake up with me?
  4. If we had one evening with no plans and no phones, how would you want it to go?
  5. Slow dance in the kitchen or a surprise at midnight?
  6. What is one thing you would like me to take the lead on?
  7. What would be a perfect night in for the two of us?

Playful dares

  1. Who would blush first if we got caught kissing in public?
  2. Who is the bigger flirt, you or me?
  3. What is the cheesiest pickup line you would actually fall for?
  4. What compliment do you secretly want to hear more of?
  5. Name three things I do that you would miss immediately.
  6. What would you wear if you wanted to make me lose my train of thought?
  7. What is something you want to try together this month?

A tip before you start

Flirty works when both people want to play. Watch for the smile. If it is not landing, change the subject with no pressure. Charm is about reading the room.

Questions people ask

How do you flirt with someone you have been with for years?

Do the small things you did at the start. Ask a playful question, hold eye contact a second longer, send a cheeky text in the middle of the day.

Are these questions explicit?

No. They are teasing and romantic, not explicit. You decide how far the conversation goes.
